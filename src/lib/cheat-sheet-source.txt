Connecting the UAV MC to wifi

Connect to the drone's MC and type:
vim /etc/network/interfaces
Press i to start editing, and edit the file to look like this:

auto lo
iface lo inet loopback
auto eth0
iface eth0 inet static
address 172.18.25.95
netmask 255.255.0.0
auto wlan0
iface wlan0 inet dhcp
wpa-ssid <name>
wpa-psk <password>
nameserver 8.8.8.8
nameserver 8.8.4.4

Replace <name> and <password> with the WiFi details.
When you finish editing, press Esc, then type :wq and press Enter.
After that run ifup wlan0 and it should work.
ping google.com to verify the internet is working.

DETAILED PROCEDURES ( FOR NEW EMPLOYEES/TRAINEES )

How to Hard Reset a station:

To turn OFF the station : 

1. Left to right turn off all breakers near the battery docks.
2. Right to left turn off until the UPS( including UPS) . Then turn off UPS buttons Right to left. The UPS buttons are in the centre. Then turn on remaining buttons in first row.
3. Then right to left turn off all buttons in the second row as well.

To turn ON the station: 

1. Left to right turn on all the buttons of first row until the UPS( Including UPS) . Then turn on the UPS from left to right. Then on the extreme left side of the UPS there is a reset button. Press the reset button, keep holding it until the beep sound.
2. Left to right turn on remaining buttons of first row, then turn on buttons of second row from left to right.
3. Turn on the buttons of battery docks row from right to left.

Related to connection, and taking the drone outside of UAV monthly check.

-  take the batteries out and using battery jig, check the batteries using BMS.

-  ( VERY IMPORTANT) after putting the payload and batteries back, make sure to click replace battery, replace payload in the DS.

-  After drone is put back inside manually, close all the doors. Make sure battery is removed before putting it in.
-  make sure there is no loose tool kept in the box where movement happens.

-  Check for errors in the DS, check status of batteries, payload, arms, vision camera.
-  make sure to put the drone is redcon mode before returning the stations.

RELATED TO CLEANING 
- close all doors firmly, check the firmness of the closed doors, and then wash the station with the pressure washer. Wash top, ac area thoroughly, also wash the external cameras.
- clean the inside of the station using cloth, clean the stage, the sides, base, the batteries themselves, and surrounding areas.
- change the filters of the AC. TOTAL 5 filters are there.
- Put the station in takeoff mode and clean the railing of the doors, spray WD40 on the rails, and the holes.
